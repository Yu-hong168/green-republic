What is the product?

Ukay Ukay Summer Mix is a wholesale reference mix of second-hand summer clothing bales for buyers in the Philippines. Category availability and final assortment are confirmed in the quotation.

How much does each bale weigh?

Two packaging options are shown: 80kg per bale or 90kg per bale. Select the preferred format in your inquiry and confirm the final packing before order closing.

The reference range is US$0.90–1.10 per kg. The final quote depends on packaging, availability, destination and commercial terms.
